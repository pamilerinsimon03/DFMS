Your Rights Under NDPA 2023 & GAID 2025

As a data subject under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and General Application and Implementation Directive (GAID) 2025, you have the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing of your data

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal information for educational purposes:

  • Academic Information: Matriculation number, level, programme, session
  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, phone number
  • Demographic Information: Date of birth, gender, state of origin, marital status
  • Contact Information: Address, emergency contact details
  • Document Information: Uploaded academic documents and verification status

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on:

  • Educational Contract: Processing necessary for your enrollment and academic administration
  • Legitimate Interest: University operations, academic record keeping, and student services
  • Consent: For non-essential data such as emergency contacts and optional demographic information

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is used for:

  • Academic record management and verification
  • Document processing and authentication
  • Communication regarding your academic status
  • System administration and security
  • Compliance with educational regulations

4. Data Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • University academic departments and administration
  • Regulatory bodies as required by law
  • Service providers under strict confidentiality agreements

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Regular security assessments
  • Staff training on data protection

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for:

  • Academic Records: 5 years after graduation or withdrawal
  • System Logs: 1 year for security and audit purposes
  • Document Uploads: Until graduation plus 2 years

Data may be retained longer if required by law or for legitimate educational purposes.

8. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that may affect your personal data, we will:

  • Notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) within 72 hours (NDPA 2023 s.40)
  • Inform affected data subjects without undue delay
  • Take immediate steps to contain and remediate the breach

9. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for system functionality. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics without your consent.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the system or email.

Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal data, you can:

  1. Contact our Data Protection Officer (dpo@ui.edu.ng)
  2. File a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) pursuant to NDPA 2023 Section 46
  3. Seek judicial remedies under Nigerian law
